Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Luuv Fryxo operates the Luuv Fryxo customer app and ordering website. For privacy questions, contact praveen2too@gmail.com.

Information we use

When you create an account or order, we use your phone number, name, order items, order status, and any details you choose to save, such as address, area, and order feedback. We also keep your rewards balance and transaction history. The app stores your session and cart on your device.

If you enable notifications, the app uses a device push token to deliver order updates and offers. We use Firebase Cloud Messaging for push delivery, an SMS provider for verification codes, and WhatsApp to send order messages where applicable. These providers receive the data needed to deliver those messages.

How we use and protect information

We use this information to process and track orders, manage your account and rewards, send requested verification and order messages, provide notifications you allow, and support customers. Customer passwords are stored as hashes. The app and website use HTTPS for network communication.

Retention and deletion

We keep account and order information while needed to provide the service. You can request account and associated data deletion from within the app or on the web. We verify that the request belongs to the account holder before processing it. Some transaction records may need to be retained for accounting, legal, security, or fraud-prevention reasons; we will explain any such retention when handling your request.

Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, email praveen2too@gmail.com.
